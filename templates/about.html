GET TO KNOW US

Better Websites Begin With Better Feedback.

We believe every website can become better with thoughtful human perspectives. HUMAN FEEDBACK brings creators and reviewers together to make website improvement more collaborative.

OUR STORY

A Community Built Around Honest Opinions.

Building a website takes time, creativity and hard work. But sometimes, the people who build websites need a fresh perspective to see what could be improved.

HUMAN FEEDBACK is designed to make those perspectives easier to find. It gives website creators a place to share their work and receive feedback from other people.

In return, contributors can earn credits for approved feedback and take part in a community where everyone has something valuable to share.
