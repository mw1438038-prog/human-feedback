PRICING QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

When does my Free trial start?

Your one-time 30-day Free trial starts when you successfully log in for the first time after registering.

Can I add a website on the Free plan?

Yes. The Free plan allows a maximum of one website. You need at least 1,000 available credits to submit that website.

Do Pro and Business require credits to add websites?

No. Pro and Business subscribers do not need a minimum credit balance to submit websites. Their website limits depend on their plan.

What happens when my Free trial expires?

You can still log in and access your account, but platform features remain locked until you purchase a paid plan. Your existing account data and history are retained.

Can I upgrade my plan?

You can choose a paid plan when you need additional website capacity or continued access after your trial.

Are paid subscriptions monthly?

Yes. Pro and Business are monthly plans. Payment and subscription management will be available when billing is integrated.
