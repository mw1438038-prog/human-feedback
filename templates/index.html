What is HUMAN FEEDBACK?

HUMAN FEEDBACK is a community platform where website creators exchange honest feedback and earn credits for approved contributions.

How do I earn credits?

You earn 10 credits when your submitted feedback is reviewed and approved.

How does the free trial work?

The Free plan provides a one-time 30-day trial starting with your first successful login.

Do I need credits to submit a website?

Free trial users need at least 1,000 available credits for a website submission. Pro and Business subscribers do not have this requirement.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Pro and Business subscriptions are monthly plans. Subscription management will be available through the platform.
