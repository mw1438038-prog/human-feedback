YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HUMAN FEEDBACK collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our platform.

Privacy notice: This is a general privacy policy draft. Before launch, the platform operator should verify it against the actual data collected, hosting providers, email services, cookies, retention practices, and applicable privacy laws.

1. Introduction

HUMAN FEEDBACK respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you visit or use our platform and how that information may be handled.

By using the platform, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it separately.

2. Information We May Collect

Account Information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Password credentials stored using appropriate security measures.
  • Account status and email verification information.
  • Profile details you choose to provide.

Website Information

  • Website name and URL.
  • Website descriptions and submitted details.
  • Ownership or authorization proof submitted for review.
  • Website approval, rejection, and moderation history.

Feedback Information

  • Ratings and written feedback comments.
  • Optional screenshots or supporting evidence.
  • Feedback approval and moderation status.
  • Replies, reports, and related communication.

Usage and Technical Information

  • Account activity and platform interactions.
  • Credit balances and transaction history.
  • Notifications and relevant activity records.
  • Technical logs, error information, and security records.
  • Device, browser, and IP information where collected by our services.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify email addresses and support account security.
  • Register and review submitted websites.
  • Process feedback, ratings, replies, and reports.
  • Calculate and maintain credit balances.
  • Display dashboards, analytics, and activity history.
  • Send important account and platform notifications.
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, spam, and security incidents.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve platform performance.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Public and Shared Information

Some information submitted to HUMAN FEEDBACK may be visible to other users depending on the feature and platform settings.

  • Website information may be displayed to platform users.
  • Approved feedback may be displayed on relevant website pages.
  • Website owners may be able to view feedback about their websites.
  • Replies may be visible to users who can access the relevant feedback.

Do not include passwords, private contact details, financial information, or other sensitive personal information in public feedback or screenshots.

5. Email Communications

We may use your email address for account-related communications, including:

  • Email verification.
  • Password reset instructions.
  • Important account and security notifications.
  • Responses to support or contact requests.
  • Important changes to platform policies or services.

Promotional communications, if introduced, will be handled according to applicable consent and unsubscribe requirements.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

HUMAN FEEDBACK may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality and improve the user experience.

  • Session cookies may help maintain login sessions.
  • Security-related cookies may help protect accounts and forms.
  • Preference cookies may remember selected settings where supported.
  • Analytics technologies may be used if introduced and properly configured.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent some platform features from working correctly.

See our Cookie Policy for additional information.

7. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable privacy laws require a legal basis for processing personal information, we may rely on one or more of the following:

  • Contract: To provide account and platform services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve services, where permitted by law.
  • Consent: Where you have given consent for a specific purpose.
  • Legal obligations: To comply with applicable laws and valid legal requests.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We may share information in limited circumstances, such as:

  • Service providers: Hosting, database, email delivery, security, and infrastructure providers that support the platform.
  • Other users: Information that must be displayed to provide website feedback and related platform features.
  • Legal requirements: Where disclosure is required by law or valid legal process.
  • Security and protection: Where necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, or safeguard platform rights.
  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, restructuring, or transfer, subject to applicable privacy requirements.

Service providers are expected to handle information according to appropriate contractual and security requirements.

9. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

Security measures may include:

  • Protected account authentication.
  • Access controls and permissions.
  • Secure connections where supported.
  • Restricted access to administrative functions.
  • Monitoring and security-related logging.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also protect their passwords and devices.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account management, platform operation, security, dispute handling, and legal compliance.

When a user's Free Trial expires, account information and history may remain stored even though access to platform features becomes restricted.

Information may be deleted, anonymized, or retained for a longer period where required or permitted by law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a copy of information where data portability rights apply.
  • Submit a complaint to a relevant privacy authority.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

12. Children's Privacy

HUMAN FEEDBACK is not designed for children who are below the minimum age required to use the platform under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable legal requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

13. International Data Transfers

Our service providers or infrastructure may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required by law, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers of personal information.

14. Third-Party Services

The platform may rely on third-party services for hosting, databases, email delivery, security, or other technical functions.

These providers may process information on our behalf to deliver their services. Their own privacy policies may also apply to information they collect directly.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our platform, service providers, data practices, or legal requirements.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

16. Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy request, please contact us through our contact page.
