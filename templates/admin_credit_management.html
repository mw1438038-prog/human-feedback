Credit Management Guidelines

Important rules for administrator adjustments.

Manual Additions

Positive adjustments add credits to the selected user's account.

Manual Deductions

Negative adjustments deduct credits. The balance cannot go below zero.

Reason Required

Every manual adjustment must include a reason, which is saved with the transaction.

Transaction Records

Successful adjustments are recorded in the credit transaction history and admin audit log.
