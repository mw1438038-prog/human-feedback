Moderation Guidelines

Use report actions carefully. Every review action is recorded in the audit log.

Under Review

Use this when more investigation is needed. The report remains open for further review.

Dismiss

Use this when the report does not require further action.

Resolve

Use this when the report has been handled without a specific removal or suspension action.

Take Action

Depending on the target, an administrator can warn a user, remove reported feedback or suspend a website.
