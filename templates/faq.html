01. Getting Started

HUMAN FEEDBACK is a platform where website owners can receive feedback from other people and contributors can earn credits for approved feedback submissions.
Users create accounts, explore eligible websites, share constructive feedback and manage their own websites. Submitted feedback goes through an approval process before credits are awarded.
Yes. You need an account to access the platform's member features, manage websites and submit feedback.

02. Accounts & Security

Visit the registration page, enter the requested information and follow the account verification instructions to complete registration.
Use the Forgot Password option on the login page and follow the password reset instructions sent to your registered email address.
Yes. You can manage available profile information through your account's profile settings.

03. Website Management

Free allows a maximum of 1 website, Pro allows up to 3 websites, and Business allows unlimited websites.
Free users need at least 1,000 available credits when submitting a website. Pro and Business users do not have this minimum credit requirement.
Yes. Website submissions require proof and are subject to manual administrative review before approval.
If a website submission is rejected, the credits reserved for that submission are refunded once according to the platform's credit rules.

04. Feedback

There is no lifetime limit on feedback entries. However, you can have only one pending feedback submission for the same website at a time. After an approved submission, a 24-hour cooldown applies before submitting again for that website.
Yes. Website owners are allowed to submit feedback on their own websites, subject to the platform's normal feedback rules and approval process.
A submission includes five ratings, each scored from 1 to 5, and a written comment. A screenshot may also be attached where applicable.
Feedback submissions are reviewed by an administrator. Credits are awarded only after the feedback is approved.

05. Credits & Rewards

Each approved feedback submission earns 10 credits. The reward is granted once for each approved submission.
No. Credits are awarded only after an administrator approves the submitted feedback.
Your account's Credits section provides information about your available balance and credit transaction history.

06. Plans & Subscriptions

Your one-time 30-day Free trial starts when you successfully log in for the first time after registration.
You can continue to log in, but platform features are locked until you purchase Pro or Business. Your existing account data and history are retained.
Pro costs $9 per month and supports up to 3 websites. Business costs $24 per month and supports unlimited websites. Both plans are billed monthly.
Paid plan checkout and recurring payment functionality are not yet connected. Billing integration will be added separately.
