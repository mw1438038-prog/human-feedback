COOKIE INFORMATION

Cookie Policy

Learn how HUMAN FEEDBACK uses cookies and similar technologies to support platform functionality, security, and user experience.

Important: This is a general Cookie Policy draft. Before launch, verify it against the actual cookies, session configuration, analytics tools, and third-party services used by the website. Obtain consent wherever applicable law requires it.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website may store on your device when you visit it. They help websites remember information about your visit and support certain website functions.

Similar technologies, such as local storage and session-based identifiers, may also be used for functionality and security.

2. Why We Use Cookies

HUMAN FEEDBACK may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping users signed in during an active session.
  • Supporting account authentication.
  • Protecting forms and platform features against certain attacks.
  • Remembering user preferences where supported.
  • Understanding website performance if analytics tools are introduced.

3. Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies support functions necessary for the website to operate, such as account sessions, authentication, and security.

Session Cookies

Session cookies are generally temporary and may be removed when you close your browser or when the relevant session expires.

Preference Cookies

These cookies may remember settings or preferences to make future visits more convenient.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics services are introduced, they may use cookies or similar technologies to help understand website usage, traffic patterns, and performance. Such technologies will be configured according to applicable consent requirements.

Third-Party Cookies

External services integrated into the platform may use their own cookies or similar technologies, subject to their own policies and settings.

4. Cookies and Account Security

When you log in to HUMAN FEEDBACK, session-related technologies may help maintain your authenticated session and protect access to your account.

You should use a secure device, avoid sharing your login details, and log out when using a shared or public computer.

5. Cookie Management

Most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through their settings.

Common browser options include:

  • Blocking cookies from selected websites.
  • Deleting stored cookies and site data.
  • Blocking third-party cookies.
  • Receiving notifications when cookies are stored, where supported.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent login sessions, security features, or other platform functions from working correctly.

6. Browser Instructions

You can manage cookies through your browser's privacy or site settings. Browser instructions may vary by version.

Google Chrome

Open Settings, select Privacy and security, and choose the available third-party cookie or site-data controls.

Mozilla Firefox

Open Settings, select Privacy & Security, and review the Enhanced Tracking Protection and cookie settings.

Microsoft Edge

Open Settings, select Cookies and site permissions, and review the available cookie controls.

Apple Safari

Open Safari privacy settings and review the available options for cookies and website data.

7. Cookie Consent

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will seek consent before using those technologies.

Essential technologies necessary to provide a service you request may be treated differently under applicable law.

You may be able to change or withdraw your consent through available website controls or browser settings.

8. How Long Cookies Remain

Some cookies are temporary and expire when a browser session ends. Others may remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.

The duration depends on the purpose and configuration of the relevant cookie or technology.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our technology, integrations, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies or similar technologies, please contact the HUMAN FEEDBACK team.
